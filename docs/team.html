Our Operational Scale

A Unified Force of 11 Elite Legal Practitioners

Choge Advocates' cross-border practice in East Africa is powered by an elite team of eleven world-class lawyers. These lawyers are organized into four professional tiers to combine academic rigor with process-oriented flexibility. From three senior partners specializing in high-profile shareholder protection and corporate governance cases, to our senior associates, advocates, and promising legal interns at all career stages, our agile two-tier structure scales rapidly to meet complex regional needs. Located in the heart of Nairobi's business district, this integrated network seamlessly develops rigorous cross-border technology due diligence, strategic corporate asset protection, and effective infrastructure security solutions for multinational institutional clients.
